Privacy Policy — qmpo Chrome Extension

Last updated: 2026-03-22

Overview

qmpo ("the Extension") converts file:// links on web pages to directory:// links so that clicking them opens the corresponding folder in your operating system's file manager. The Extension is open-source under the MIT License.

Data Collection

The Extension does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. Specifically:

  • No browsing history, page content, or URLs are sent to any external server.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or tracking of any kind is included.
  • No cookies are set or read by the Extension.
  • No user accounts or authentication are required.

Local Storage

The Extension uses chrome.storage.sync solely to persist your preferences (enabled/disabled state, folder-icon indicator toggle, and domain allowlist/blocklist). This data is synced across your Chrome profile by Chrome's built-in sync mechanism and is never sent to any third-party service.

Permissions

Permission Why it is needed
<all_urls> The content script must run on every page to detect file:// links. No page content is collected or transmitted.
storage Save and sync user preferences across devices.
activeTab Interact with the currently active tab.
tabs Create a short-lived hidden tab to trigger the directory:// protocol handler, then close it automatically.

Third-Party Services

The Extension does not communicate with any external servers or third-party services.
